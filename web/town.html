            How the hosted town works

            • Testnet only. No real money. The house pays each clone's 0.5 NEAR stake with testnet NEAR.
            • No login. You get a guest account (g….clonestown.testnet) and a link. This browser remembers it.
            • The leash is real. Each clone is a NEAR sub-account of your guest whose only key is a function-call key for the market. It can trade. It can never withdraw: if it tries, the chain refuses the transaction.
            • Once a day. Around 02:00 UTC the keeper plays the night: twelve hours of trading, then dawn. Each clone settles, then merges back into your guest account (a real DeleteAccount), and its result stays on chain.
