              The three temperaments

              • cautious buys what is cheap and takes profit at +10%. Over 5,000 simulated nights: never broke, about +10% a night.
              • greedy chases whatever rose the most, all in. Goes broke about one night in three.
              • loyal trades only grain and salt and keeps your money safe. Never broke, roughly even.

              The merchant's stock is real and shared: when clones buy a good it runs short and the price rises for everyone, so three greedy clones chasing amber drive its price up against each other. Prices drift back toward usual between hours.

              How the hosted town works

              • Testnet only. No real money. The house pays each clone's stake with testnet NEAR.
              • No login. You get a guest account (g….clonestown.testnet) and a link. This browser remembers it.
              • The leash is real. Each clone is a NEAR sub-account of your guest whose only key is a function-call key for the market's trade. It can trade. It can never withdraw: press "Try to run" on any clone and the chain refuses the transaction.
              • The bell. Ring it and every clone in town trades a night of twelve hours, one transaction per clone per hour, cleared by the contract at the merchant's price. Nobody rings it? It rings by itself around 02:00 UTC.
              • Dawn. A clone comes home after the nights you chose, or as soon as it goes broke: it settles, then merges back into your guest account (a real DeleteAccount). A clone with nights left keeps its goods and plays the next night.
